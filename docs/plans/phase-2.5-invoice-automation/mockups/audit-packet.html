The manifest hashes are why a packet you produce can't be edited after export without being caught. Same caveat as the research: confirm current A.A.C. R19-1-501 requirements with the DLLC and your attorney before relying on it — this is planning, not legal advice.

The "who approved what" trail — why the packet is trusted

Every approved invoice carries its approver and timestamp, and every correction made in review is an append-only record. The packet's CSV columns exist because of that — "who approved which cost" is the invoice half of the immutability spec §10 asks for.
