New Invoice

Snap a photo or forward the email, and the line items come back.

Three intake paths:

  1. Photo: use the phone camera.
  2. File: select a PDF from your device.
  3. Email forward: forward invoices to invoices@truestock.app.

What happens next: the invoice enters the review queue. Each line is flagged with why it's waiting (price jump, duplicate, doesn't add up, unmatched item).
