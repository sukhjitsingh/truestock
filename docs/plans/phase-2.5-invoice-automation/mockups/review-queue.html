The typing that matters — exceptions are typed, not just counted

The queue never just says "3 need attention." It says what kind of attention, so a month's invoices can be cleared in one sitting:

BadgeWhat it meansWhat you do
price jumpUnit cost differs from what you paid beforeLook once, accept or flag to your rep
unmatched itemA line the app can't map to your catalogMap it once — the next invoice knows it
doesn't add upLines don't equal the printed totalFind the dropped line — the silent under-cost
possible duplicateSame vendor + invoice number already enteredConfirm it's new, or dismiss
missing pack sizeA case line without a readable pack sizeType 12 or 24 once
State: nothing waiting
Empty state: "You're all caught up. New invoices land here the moment they're captured." The month-end target is met when this screen is empty — that is the definition of done.
State: a document is mid-reading
In progress: a row in reading shows vendor and date as soon as known, no badges yet, and a "this usually takes under a minute" hint. It appears in the queue immediately — nothing is captured and hidden.
