Design decisions

  • Chose "Bacardí Superior Rum" as the worked example specifically because it has one barcode already (captured via scan-to-enroll) and no cost/case size yet — the common state for most of the catalog, not the exception.
  • Pricing is its own <section>, toggled entirely on/off by the demo role switch — not opacity, not a lock icon over a visible field. A manager viewing this product has that section missing from the DOM, matching how the real form would be composed per role.
  • Barcodes are a table (one-to-many), not a single "UPC" field — matches ProductBarcode's real shape (barcode, format, pack_level, is_primary) and the invariant that scan-to-enroll only ever inserts one row into it, never a column on product.
  • Deactivate is a distinct, clearly-labeled danger-zone action with copy that states explicitly what it does and does not do (no delete) — directly answering invariant 6 rather than using ambiguous iconography.
  • Waste factor is editable here but explained inline, since it's meaningful for kegs only (0.100 default) and inert for everything else (0.000) — a field most editors will never touch.
  • Par level/reorder point sit on this form even though "par scope" (per-product vs per-location) is an open question in CLAUDE.md — this write path already matches the documented interim behavior (write null-location rows only).

Open questions

  • Reactivation isn't specced. The form only offers deactivate. If an owner needs to bring a product back (relist a seasonal spirit), does that go through this same form (an "Active" toggle appears once inactive) or a separate confirmation flow? Left as a single one-way action for now.
  • Barcode edit vs. re-scan. Should the back office allow typing a barcode by hand (as drawn here), or must every barcode originate from an actual scan to avoid typos creating an unresolvable code? The form as built assumes manual entry is fine for corrections.
  • Wine producer field has no column yet. CLAUDE.md flags that Merlot/Chardonnay "need a producer before they can be costed or scanned," but db/schema.ts has no producer/vintage column on product — editing a wine today would still only offer name/brand/category. This form doesn't invent that field since it isn't in the schema; flagging it here rather than guessing its shape.
